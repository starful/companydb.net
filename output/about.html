About CompanyDB

CompanyDB turns public company filings from Japan, Korea, and the US into plain-English pages for personal investors — statements, multi-year trends, and note-driven takeaways from EDINET, DART, and SEC EDGAR.

What you get

  • Company pages with consolidated statements and a short investor takeaway
  • An index you can search, plus a simple multi-company trend compare
  • Coverage that grows from a curated universe — not every listed company yet
